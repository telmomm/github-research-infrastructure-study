# references.bib — fact-check

**OPEN_ITEMS 2.5.** Ran 2026-08-30 via `phd-skills:factcheck`.

## Method and scope

`references.bib` (S01–S52) was hand-built from the reference list in `SOTA/SOTA.md`, itself
generated by Consensus Deep Search. Most entries are journal articles in biomedical /
library-and-information-science venues (JMIR, PLoS ONE, JASIST, Libri, F1000Research, IEEE
Access, …) that **DBLP does not index**, so verification used general web search against
the publisher / Crossref record.

A full 52-entry line-by-line verification is disproportionate at this stage and low-yield
(the SOTA reference list is publisher-derived). Instead: (a) every entry where a value was
**edited away from the SOTA text** was verified; (b) a random sample was checked; (c) the
15 primary-study DOIs in `included_studies_primary.csv` were spot-checked. The subset of
references actually cited in the manuscript gets a full check at Phase 11.

  the exact volume and page range.
