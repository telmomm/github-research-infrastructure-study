### Headline descriptives (Track B)

- Strand: SWf 13, RIS 10, PROV 7, RDM 6, OS 5, EMERG 4, COORD 3, VRE 2, VC 2.
- Lifecycle focus: execution 22, cross-cutting 17, outputs 7, upstream 3, coordination 3.
- Lifecycle-stage attention: 7 of 15 stages thin (idea, question, planning, ethics, post-publication traceability, cross-stage coordination, governance); 4 heavy (data collection, processing, analysis, dissemination).
- Requirement attention: only 3 of 17 requirement rows well covered; upstream planning / question-tracking / decision-history all weak-gap.

### Gap statement carried into the manuscript

The evidence base shows a strong normative consensus on what research infrastructure should do, but the field is output-biased and thin on integrated, lifecycle-wide management — especially upstream planning, decision history and cross-stage coordination. This positions the study in the gap between artifact hosting and end-to-end research-process management.
