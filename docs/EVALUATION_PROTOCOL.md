## Objective

Evaluate whether the proposed GitHub-based framework supports research management requirements.

---

# Evaluation dimensions

## 1. Requirement coverage

Does the framework support the identified research management requirements?

---

## 2. Traceability

Can research activities and decisions be traced?

---

## 3. Documentation

Are relevant research processes documented?

---

## 4. Organization

Can research artifacts and tasks be systematically organized?

---

## 5. Transparency

Can project progress and research development be understood?

---

## 6. Usability

Can researchers practically implement the framework?
