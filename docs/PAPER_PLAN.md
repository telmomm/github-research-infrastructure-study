# Paper Plan

# From Code Repository to Research Infrastructure: Evaluating GitHub for Managing the Scientific Research Lifecycle

---

# 1. Study concept

## Central idea

Scientific research projects are frequently managed using fragmented combinations of local folders, cloud storage services, email, spreadsheets, task management tools, analysis scripts, and manuscript preparation platforms.

This study investigates whether GitHub can be used beyond its conventional role as a software development platform to provide a structured environment for managing scientific research activities across the research lifecycle.

The proposed approach treats GitHub as a potential research infrastructure supporting:

- research planning;
- task management;
- documentation;
- collaboration;
- decision traceability;
- version control;
- research provenance;
- automation;
- research output management.

---

# 2. Research problem

Modern research projects generate multiple interconnected artifacts:

```text
Research question
        ↓
Protocol
        ↓
Literature
        ↓
Data
        ↓
Analysis
        ↓
Results
        ↓
Manuscript
        ↓
Publication
```

These activities are frequently distributed across multiple disconnected systems.

```text
Email
Excel
Local folders
Cloud storage
Word documents
Analysis scripts
Task managers
Reference managers
```

This fragmentation may reduce:

- traceability;
- transparency;
- documentation;
- version consistency;
- research provenance;
- project visibility.

---

# 3. Research gap

Existing research has extensively investigated:

- reproducible research;
- research software;
- Open Science;
- research data management;
- scientific workflows;
- project management.

However, limited evidence is available regarding the systematic use of GitHub as an integrated infrastructure for managing scientific research projects across multiple stages of the research lifecycle.

The study therefore investigates whether GitHub capabilities can be systematically mapped to research management requirements and organized into a reusable framework.

---

# 4. Study objective

The objective of this study is to develop and evaluate a GitHub-based framework for managing scientific research projects across the research lifecycle.

---

# 5. Research questions

## RQ1

What research project management requirements are identified in the scientific literature?

## RQ2

To what extent can GitHub functionalities support research project management requirements?

## RQ3

Can a structured GitHub-based framework improve the traceability, organization, documentation, and transparency of research projects compared with fragmented research workflows?

## RQ4

Which stages of the scientific research lifecycle benefit most from GitHub-based research management?
